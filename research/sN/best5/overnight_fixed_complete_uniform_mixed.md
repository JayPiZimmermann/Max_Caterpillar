# A uniform obstruction to proportional interior crossings

**Status:** written proof independently reviewed by the parent. The two
elementary algebraic controls in `overnight_fixed_complete_mixed_controls.lean`
are Lean verified; the geometric theorem below is not formalized in Lean.
The earlier five-coordinate proof and all archived certificates are preserved.

Let N >= 5, and let V be the span of the linear functions and
sigma(x_i-x_j), i<j, on R^N. Suppose

    max_N = R + sum_k d_k sigma(g_k),

where R,g_k belong to V, each g_k is invariant under common translation,
each d_k is nonzero, and K=2 or3. Then there is no braid chamber on which
**every** g_k is strictly mixed.
Here strictly mixed means taking both positive and negative values in the
open chamber. Two or three mixed units must all be proportional because
every interior crease needs at least two units for cancellation.

## Proof

Fix a chamber order pi(1),...,pi(N), let S_j be its first j labels, and
write t_j=x_pi(j)-x_pi(j+1)>0. Put v_k(S)=g_k(1_S). Common-translation
invariance gives

    g_k(x) = sum_(j=1)^(N-1) t_j v_k(S_j),
    v_k(empty)=v_k([N])=0.

Each v_k is a Boolean polynomial of degree at most two, since
sigma(1_S(i)-1_S(j))=1_S(i)(1-1_S(j)).

Consider the face on which the highest three labels tie, and every lower
label is strictly ordered. Modulo common shifts its relative interior is
the positive cone generated by 1_S3,...,1_S(N-1). At least one g_k must
vanish identically on this entire face. Otherwise their zero sets are a
finite union of proper linear hyperplanes in that face, so there is a
relative-interior point where every g_k is nonzero. All outer ReLUs then
linearize locally. The resulting germ is shallow in the three tied
coordinates, whereas max of three coordinates is not shallow: its jump
on a fixed coordinate-equality plane is nonzero when that pair is highest
and zero when the third coordinate is higher. A shallow function has a
constant consolidated jump on each of its hyperplanes. This is a
contradiction.

Thus some k has v_k(S_j)=0 for every j>=3. Proportionality and strict
mixedness make this true for every k. Each preactivation on the chamber
therefore has the form

    A_k t_1 + B_k t_2,

with A_k,B_k nonzero and opposite in sign. Its zero wall meets the
relative interior of every facet t_j=0 for j>=3, and changes sign on that
facet. Continuity makes all g_k strictly mixed in the adjacent chamber.
If the proportionality hypothesis continues there, the same face argument
again kills all chain coefficients from the third onward.

For the K=2 and K=3 consequences below, proportionality does continue:
every interior crease needs at least two nonzero units, and two or three
strictly mixed units can only cancel by sharing a single wall. More
generally the stated arbitrary-K claim requires this continuation on the
chambers reached by the bottom-label swaps; proportionality in the initial
chamber alone does not supply it for K>=4.

For K=2 or3, propagate through all permutations of the bottom N-2 labels,
keeping A=S_2 fixed. Every nonempty T contained in those bottom labels is
a prefix of some such order, so

    v_k(A union T)=0.

The full-set value follows from translation invariance. Choose any three
bottom labels. On their Boolean three-cube the seven values indexed by
nonempty T are zero. The third mixed difference of v_k is zero because
v_k has degree at most two. Hence its eighth value v_k(A) is zero as
well, contradicting B_k!=0.

Consequently the uniform conclusion holds for K=2 or3. For arbitrary K,
the same proof holds under the stronger hypothesis that all forms remain
proportional and strictly mixed throughout the connected collection of
chambers generated by permuting the bottom labels. No unrestricted
arbitrary-K assertion is made.

## Consequences and scope

For K=2, a single mixed unit cannot cancel its crease, and both mixed
units are excluded. Every individual outer fold is therefore linear on
each braid chamber. Grillo, Hertrich, and Loho's conforming-function
theorem excludes max_N for N>=5. Equivalently, their Proposition B.2
places each conforming fold of a Boolean quadratic in Boolean degree at
most four, whereas max_N has nonzero degree-N mixed difference. This
also allows the shallow remainder R.

For K=3, every chamber has either zero or two mixed preactivations. In
the latter case their output signs are opposite; after output magnitudes
are absorbed into the preactivations, their chamber-linear forms are
equal or opposite. The remaining preactivation has a weak fixed sign.
This justifies the complete closed pair-cancellation disjunction used
by the five-coordinate QF_LRA model. Identically zero chamber forms are
weak fixed-sign forms and do not create an omitted case.

The argument is restricted to the complete comparison span V. Arbitrary
first hidden layer directions need not give quadratic Boolean values or
linear restrictions on braid chambers. No new unrestricted second-width
lower bound follows.

Primary reference: Grillo, Hertrich, and Loho,
[Depth-Bounds for Neural Networks via the Braid Arrangement, Theorem 5.2
and Proposition B.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.09324v2#S5).
