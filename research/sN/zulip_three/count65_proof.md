Signed reconstruction has A=L and B=-L; split/hybrid reconstruction has pointwise nonnegative A and B. On the exact reconstruction fiber these are complementary closed conditions. Equivalently, the signed test is A+B=0, whereas the other test is A,B≥0. Both are conditions on actual readout functions on the entire real domain.

Continuous canonical sections and explicit paths inside each fiber prove that these two sectors stay connected over the whole varying residual parameter family. This step does not infer a total-space count by multiplying the count of one representative fiber. It is shared by the mixed and star proofs in `HeadTopology.lean`.

## Why arbitrary biases do not change the count

Simultaneously multiplying every bias by t gives f_t(x)=t*f(x/t) for t>0. The target MAX4 is positively homogeneous, so this deformation preserves exactness; continuity handles t=0. Bias removal commutes with every allowed physical symmetry. It therefore induces the proved equivalence of connected components on the genuine symmetry quotients, without assuming path lifting.
