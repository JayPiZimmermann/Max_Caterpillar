# Direct six-case cover for the same-label three-root sector

Date: 3 November 2026. This is a mathematical support-cover argument,
with an independent finite replay. It replaces the large support inventory
for this particular sector; it does not exclude the remaining sectors.

Consider

    MAX5 = ell + R + ReLU(g) + ReLU(h),

with R,g,h in the span of seven invariant physical ReLU features.
At q0=-e0 only g vanishes, at q1=-e1 only h vanishes, and both vanish
at q2,q3,q4. Assume the unique-contact matchings have the same
distinguished shared label. Normalize their signs by complementing cores
and adjusting R, and label the mandatory root planes 02,12,34. Suppose
these are the only three physical root rows; the other four nonzero
normals have exact supports of size at least three.

## Every shared contact has at least four singular physical rows

Suppose at most three physical first rows vanish at a shared q_i.
The local positive-two-core MAX4 theorem for at most three distinct
first hyperplanes gives at least one effective canonical comparator
germ. A core already canonical at its unique contact can be canonical
at q_i only with a matching containing an additional root:

| Shared contact | Additional root for g | Additional root for h |
| --- | --- | --- |
| q2 | 01 | 01 |
| q3 | 04 | 14 |
| q4 | 03 | 13 |

To see the matching restriction, its edge avoiding both the old and
new omitted labels is visible in the old comparator germ. It must be
that old matching's edge avoiding the new omitted label. The other
edge is then forced. This uses consolidated root curvature, so it
does not assume a choice of physical feature orientation. None of the
additional roots in the table exists. The supposed canonical germ is
impossible, and each shared contact therefore has at least four
singular physical rows. No three-contact budget or injective assignment
argument is used.

At q2 only root 34 is singular; at q3 and q4 exactly roots 02 and 12 are
singular. Consequently the four nonroot supports omit coordinate 2 at
least three times, coordinate 3 at least twice, and coordinate 4 at
least twice. They have at least seven omissions in total among {2,3,4}.

## Seven omissions give exactly six support orbits

A support of size at least three omits at most two members of {2,3,4}.
Four such supports therefore have seven or eight omissions. A support
with two omissions is exactly 01c for c in {2,3,4}.

With eight omissions all four supports have this form. Write n_c for
the number equal to 01c. Then n2<=1, n3<=2, n4<=2, and their sum is 4.
Up to exchanging 3 and 4, the possibilities are (n2,n3,n4)=(1,2,1) and
(0,2,2).

With seven omissions, three supports are of the form 01c and the fourth
omits exactly one shared label. If that label is 2, the three counts must
be (1,1,1). The fourth support is 034,134, or 0134; exchanging 0 and 1 leaves
two possibilities. If the omitted label is 3, the counts are (0,2,1).
The fourth support is 024,124, or 0124; again exchanging 0 and 1 leaves two
possibilities. Omitting 4 gives the same cases after exchanging 3 and 4.

Thus the six representatives are exactly:

The only symmetry used is generated by swapping 0 with 1 (and g with h)
and by swapping 3 with 4. Repeated supports retain arbitrary distinct,
proportional, or opposite real normals. Actual coefficients and feature
orientations have not been restricted by this argument.

3876 four-row multisets. The three omission inequalities alone leave 12
labelled multisets, with exactly the six orbits above. It imports no
old producer or support inventory. It separately checks that every
survivor satisfies all C3/C4 support conditions, then matches the six
selected model metadata files. C3/C4 are therefore redundant in these
six exact support models and are unnecessary for this cover.

The geometry dependency is the local positive-two-core MAX4 rigidity
theorem with at most three first hyperplanes, plus compatibility with
the two unique comparator germs. The existing proofs are recorded in
`notes/max5_exact/local4_matching_extremality_audit.md`,
`notes/max5_exact/local4_one_root_audit.md`, and
`notes/max5_exact/three_shared_one_each_cover_audit.md`.
The original 387-case cover and the eight-row three-comparator budget
are not needed to establish this six-case result.
The local MAX4 rigidity theorem still uses its independently checked
288-case one-root/two-nonroot CPC certificate. Removing the large support
inventory does not remove all upstream computer assistance.

Combined with the six selected source-bound CPC refutations and Ethos
replays, this eliminates the entire same-label sector with exactly
three physical root rows. It does not eliminate its sectors with four
or more physical root rows, another contact pattern, or all width (9,4)
