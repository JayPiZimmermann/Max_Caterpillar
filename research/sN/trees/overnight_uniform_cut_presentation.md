# Charged cuts and the first triangular correction

25 October 2026. This note isolates the remaining presentation problem after
the exact shifted-minus residue factorization. It proves a local correction
and a fixed-mark lifting rule. It does not prove the needed global spanning
or outward-twin separator theorem.

## Fixed marks make factor relations lift

For an inward edge with child component C, child endpoint v and outside
endpoint h, put q=sum_C omega. On q=a, its residue factors into a canonical
C kernel (apex -a, root v, reversed orientations) and a canonical D kernel
(apex a, original root, hub h shifted by+a), times (-1)^|C|a².

The two frequency blocks are independent over Q(a). Therefore the kernel
of the evaluation map from a tensor product of the two formal factor
spaces is generated by factor relations. Equivalently, choose bases for
the two actual rational-function spans; products of independent rational
functions in disjoint variable sets are independent. This elementary
tensor statement alone forgets the attachment marks.

If the C root v is fixed across a relation, that relation lifts away from
q=a without alteration: every rooted cut excludes v, so the displayed
kernel is independent of omega_v before imposing the frequency-sum
relation. One may change omega_v to adjust the total charge q while
leaving the apex parameter a and every edge factor fixed.

If the D attachment h is fixed, its relation also lifts: off the residue,
the uniform substitution omega_h -> omega_h+q gives the correct balanced
D frequencies and reproduces each old child cut. Thus a relation valid
for all balanced D frequencies remains valid after this common shift.

One may work over Q(a) for the tensor argument. All factors in either
fixed-size block have the same homogeneity, so after scaling all block
frequencies by a, their linear relation space is the extension of the
constant-coefficient relation space. This observation avoids claiming
that arbitrary rational coefficient functions are allowed dictionary
coefficients.

Relations mixing the C root or D attachment do not automatically lift.
The global kernels are their charged extensions, while the scalar lower
relation is only known after the charge is specialized to a. The two
extensions can differ by a multiple of q-a, which survives after the
boundary factor1/[q(q-a)] is restored.

## Exact correction for a root slide

Put f_+(x)=1/[x(x+a)] and f_-(x)=1/[x(x-a)]. If a detached component is
split into two blocks with charges x,y and q=x+y, the simplest root slide
identifies f_-(x) with f_-(y) only on q=a. Its full defect is

    f_-(x)-f_-(y)
      = (y-x)(q-a)/[xy(x-a)(y-a)].

After restoring the inward boundary factor f_-(q), the q-a pole cancels.
The exact six-term identity gives a correction entirely in canonical
rooted tree factors:

    f_-(q)[f_-(x)-f_-(y)]
      = f_+(q)[f_+(x)-f_+(y)]
        - f_+(x)f_-(y) + f_-(x)f_+(y).

The old inward cut q has been replaced by an outward cut q, or by proper
inward cuts x,y. All internal factors of arbitrarily attached blocks
remain common, so this is a uniform grafted identity, not a small-arity
interpolation. The adjacent checker clears the exact polynomial
denominators for both identities using integer arithmetic.

This is the first triangular correction, not a complete rewriting system.
To complete an elimination proof one would need a presentation theorem
showing that every mixed-root and mixed-attachment residue relation is
generated by fixed-mark relations and controlled corrections of this
kind. In addition, one must show that these corrections preserve the
outward-twin ideal, including the exceptional head positions. Neither
assertion is established here.

## The endpoint must permit inward leaves

Eliminating nonsingleton shifted-minus poles cannot force every remaining
unit to be all outward. Already at N5, the star with root2 and edges
(2,1),(3,2),(4,2) has only an inward singleton edge. It has no nonsingleton
shifted-minus pole, but its symmetric leaf-double-wheel residue
8/[ab(a-b)] is independent of the all-outward star residue recorded in
`overnight_uniform_doublewheel.md`.

A more plausible endpoint class permits inward leaf edges, with all
internal edges outward relative to the effective root. Even the claim
that every pole-free combination in the outward-twin span reduces to
this endpoint span remains open. The exact seven-label witness in the
double-wheel note still has internal shifted-minus poles and therefore
does not refute this endpoint claim.

The bounded N7 gate has92 literal outward-twin orbit columns. On the
existing exact-frequency rows reduced modulo1000003, their rank is47.

ranks for subset sizes2,3,4,5 are8,12,6,12, with combined rank35; adjoining
all residue rows to the original rows still gives rank47. Thus the
observed kernel has precisely the endpoint dimension47-35=12. The complete

finite-field diagnostic, not a rational spanning proof.

## Ambient elimination versus preservation of the tree span

For the complete simple-fraction space, zero residues do permit removal
of a pole hyperplane: this is the exact sequence in Proposition14(1) of
[Brion--Vergne, *Arrangement of hyperplanes I*](https://arxiv.org/html/math/9903178).
Every marked coordinate multiple omega_j R_U belongs to that space.
Iterating the sequence therefore removes all forbidden hyperplanes in
the ambient logarithmic space. It does not preserve the restricted span
of outward-twin tree kernels. Establishing that preservation, or replacing
it by a controlled larger matching ideal, remains the substantive task.
