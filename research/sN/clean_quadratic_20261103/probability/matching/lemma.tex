\begin{lemma}[A random matching estimate]\label{lem:random-matching-estimate}
Let $G$ be a graph with $e$ edges on $m\ge 2$ vertices, let $0\le 2J\le m$,
let $M$ be a uniformly random matching of size $J$, and put
$X=|M\cap E(G)|$.
For $0<\theta\le 1$,
\[
 \mathbb E\theta^X
 \le \exp\!\left(-\frac{2Je(1-\theta^3)}{3m(m-1)}\right).
\]
In particular, $\mathbb E2^{-X}\le \exp(-Je/(2m^2))$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The cases $e=0$ or $J=0$ are immediate. Generate $M$ by a uniform
permutation, pairing its first $2J$ positions consecutively. For each
$ab\in E(G)$, swap $a$ into the first position and then $b$ into the
second. The resulting permutation is uniform subject to these two
positions: every such permutation has exactly $m(m-1)$ preimages, as one
sees by undoing the swaps. After forgetting the pair order, the resulting
matching therefore has the conditional law of $M$ given $ab\in M$.
Write $X_{ab}$ for its number of graph edges. Only three pairs can change,
so $X_{ab}\le X+3$.

Set $F(z)=\mathbb E z^X$ and $p=\mathbb P(ab\in M)=2J/(m(m-1))$.
Expanding $X$ as a sum of edge indicators, for $0<z\le 1$ we obtain
\[
 F'(z)=p\sum_{ab\in E(G)}\mathbb E z^{X_{ab}-1}
 \ge epz^2F(z).
\]
Integrating $F'/F\ge epz^2$ from $\theta$ to $1$, and using $F(1)=1$,
gives the claim.
\end{proof}
